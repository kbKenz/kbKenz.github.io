Bektur Kenzhebaev

I am a computer science student at the American Institute of Technology, Salymbekov University, a research collaborator with ARCIDS Lab at Wuhan University, and a founding AI engineer at Verc. My work connects machine learning research with the engineering of production AI systems.

I am interested in how intelligent systems learn, make decisions, interact, and improve, with interests spanning machine learning, AI safety, robotics, and recommender systems. My current research explores evaluation, failure attribution, multi-agent behavior, and auditable memory in agentic AI.

I am preparing for thesis-based graduate study beginning Fall 2027. I welcome research collaborations across machine learning, AI safety, robotics, recommender systems, and related areas. If you see a potential fit with your group or project, I would be glad to hear from you.
